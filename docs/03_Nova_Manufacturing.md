# Company Overview

Nova Manufacturing Group is a medium-sized industrial manufacturing company specializing in precision mechanical equipment for automotive, aerospace, and heavy machinery industries.

The company operates multiple factories and warehouses across different regions while managing suppliers, inventory, production, maintenance, logistics, and quality assurance.

Nova Manufacturing serves hundreds of enterprise customers and processes thousands of production events every day.

# Enterprise Problems

Information is scattered across multiple systems.

Managers cannot quickly identify operational risks.

Maintenance is often reactive instead of predictive.

Business reports require manual effort.

Production delays are difficult to explain.

Documents are difficult to search.

Inventory shortages occur unexpectedly.

Machine failures create expensive downtime.

Large amounts of operational knowledge remain hidden inside documents.

---

# Business Goal

Create one intelligent platform capable of understanding the company's structured data, documents, operational events, machine data, and business knowledge to assist employees in making faster and better decisions.
